Make the team name look editable

Two pieces: a resting-state affordance for the team name, and a reusable ⓘ section tip that carries different copy per dashboard section. Everything below is live — type, save, hit a duplicate, open a tip. Section 2 has the three affordances side by side and the recommendation.

02

Three affordances, at rest

All three are shown with no hover and no tooltip — what a GM on a phone actually sees. That is the only test that matters here.

A · Captioned field Recommended
Team Management
Your team name
Team 6 Rename

Three independent signals — a caption naming the value, field chrome around it, a verb on a control. Any one of them read alone answers the question. It is also the only option where the resting state and the edit state occupy the same box, so nothing jumps on click.

B · Underline + pencil
Manage Team 6

Smallest change, keeps the heading voice. But it still asks the GM to decode decoration, the 17px icon is under the 44px touch floor, and the dashed rule reads as a spelling error to some. Keep as fallback if the header must stay one line.

C · Separate Rename button
Manage Team 6 Rename team

Unambiguous, but the name itself stays dead text, the control drifts away from the value it edits on narrow screens, and it adds a fourth button to a header that already has Copy roster emails. Reach for this only if the name must remain a link to the public team page.
